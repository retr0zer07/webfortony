About us

Small by design not by ambition

We are a freelance design studio focused on branding, logo design and visual identity.

Our approach is simple good ideas, thoughtful design and no unnecessary agency drama. We keep things direct, flexible and focused on creating work that actually works.

Working with clients worldwide, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Three languages ✦ One creative approach ✦ Zero boring design

FAQ

  • 01

    How much is a project?

    Hmm, let me ask you something first: how much is a house? It depends, right? A studio apartment is not going to cost the same as a mansion. Design works the same way. Your budget, requirements and project scope determine what we can build together.

    →
  • 02

    How long does it take to create design projects?

    It depends on what you need. Some projects can be completed in 10 days or less, while more complex ones may take up to 25 days. The timeline will always depend on scope, requirements and level of detail.

    →
  • 03

    Can you also handle larger projects?

    We are a small remote design studio, so we keep things personal and flexible. For smaller projects one designer can lead with team supervision, and for larger projects we all work together to ensure each detail gets proper attention.

    →
  • 04

    What do you need from me to get started?

    Usually, we need information about your business, goals, references you like, and any existing materials. If you prefer, we can jump on a quick video call and shape the brief together.

    →
  • 05

    Why should I work with a small design studio?

    Working with a small studio means direct communication and a more personal experience. We also provide ongoing support, so future updates stay consistent because we already know your project.
